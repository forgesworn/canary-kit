Privacy & what this protects

This native prototype is published by the Canary Kit project (ForgeSworn). It bundles Vouch on this device. It has no publisher account, server-held vault, message backend, advertising, analytics SDK or relay. Opening the separate public website or a privacy-contact link is your deliberate action and exposes ordinary request information to that service.

Connect exchanges temporary public keys, session identifiers, commitments and released random contributions directly over Bluetooth or NFC. These codes contain no private key, name or shared pair secret. Each device derives the secret locally. Nearby observers can copy public codes and correlate one session. Bluetooth can reach beyond touching distance; radio proximity does not prove identity or a meeting. Both people compare four fixed words and save separately. One device cannot prove the other human confirmed or saved. Calls later use rotating words; setup words have no countdown.

Bluetooth requires the platform’s nearby-device permission and an enabled radio. Android NFC uses a temporary emulated card while Connect is open; a native reader starts only when you choose NFC. iPhone uses an explicit Core NFC reader session for an Android peer. No GPS/location or microphone access is requested. Radio activity is restricted to Connect, stops on leaving, locking or backgrounding, and pauses after a minute; you can resume the same encrypted setup. Camera fallback decodes frames locally without recording or upload.

Moving transfer QRs reuse public invitations/receiving requests and recipient-encrypted replies/copies. They do not contain a private key or plaintext pair secret. Frames use a complete SHA-256 digest to reject incomplete/mixed data; that is not proof of the other person’s identity. Compare the fixed pair/import code together. A trusted video call can carry the QR in each direction when a second screen is available. On a single phone use the chosen chat’s public-link/encrypted-file handoff. The call, screen-sharing or recording provider and participants may see or retain the public/ciphertext frames and their timing. Share only the transfer QR; never share your vault, call words or recovery screen. Your own duress words are visible in Speak; concealment is not guaranteed. Dedicated transfer camera reading and partial assembly stop after three minutes, on leaving or on lock; nearby radios and ordinary offline Connect retain their one-minute activity pause. Selected text/JSON transfer files are decoded on this device without upload; imported people are reviewed before saving.

The encrypted local vault includes pair secrets, private labels, contact notes, pending session keys/nonces and private group signing keys and evidence. Native device unlock uses your system biometrics or device credential. The unlock seed is protected by device-only Keychain or Android Keystore storage; Vouch adds no credential cloud sync. Otherwise use a strong vault passphrase. The existing AES-GCM vault and passphrase recovery format are retained. Add a recovery passphrase and export an encrypted backup: device-only unlock is not portable recovery.

Locking clears displayed words and in-memory vault references as a best effort. Encryption protects a locked vault or copied backup; it cannot protect an unlocked compromised device, tampered app, screen capture or live relay. Both pair holders can derive either side’s words. Removing a pair or unfinished session changes the active local vault, not old backups, OS remnants or another person’s copy. No secure-erasure or remote-revocation promise is made.

Group vouches remain optional and use separate keys. Agreed group nicknames, signed statements and chosen proofs are disclosed to their intended recipients when you deliberately share them; recipients may retain or forward them. Local account observations and past checks do not automatically verify a messaging account, grant admission or provide a live withdrawal/membership service. No Nostr account or relay is required.

Recovery backups are encrypted. Public code files contain no shared secret. Device-copy files are encrypted for the receiving device. OS export/import/share destinations are chosen by you and may use the internet or retain files. Temporary export-file cleanup does not erase recipients’ copies. The older shared-secret recovery QR/manual path contains its shared secret and must stay private.

This is a prototype for device testing, not an independently audited authentication system or emergency service. Duress words need an agreed human response and send no alert. Losing this device or its app data without a usable recovery backup can lose your people.

Privacy contact

Privacy questions, complaints and rights requests can be sent privately to the Canary Kit project maintainers through GitHub’s private report route. GitHub is a separate service requiring an account. Never include live words, pairing links or unlock secrets. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. The publisher has no remote pair vault to retrieve or erase.
