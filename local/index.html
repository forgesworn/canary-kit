Privacy & what this protects

Your nicknames and pair secrets are encrypted in this browser. The vault locks when you leave the tab or after five minutes without interaction. Backups are encrypted and require your vault passphrase. No account, analytics, message service, relay, location access or microphone is used.

QR scanning uses your camera only when you ask. Frames are decoded on this device, are not recorded or uploaded, and the camera is stopped when you finish, cancel or leave. The QR contains a shared secret: keep it private.

Device unlock is optional and requires supported encryption-key functionality from your browser and passkey provider. Your provider may sync its credentials according to your platform settings. We do not receive the passkey or unlock key. The passphrase remains your recovery route.

Encryption protects a locked vault or a stolen backup. It cannot protect an unlocked compromised device, malicious browser extensions, screen capture, a tampered website or a live relay. Only use a trusted address or copy. Both holders can derive either side’s words. If a secret is exposed, pair again privately; removing a local entry does not revoke the other person’s copy.

This does not certify identity, safety or a past meeting. Duress needs an agreed human response; no emergency service is provided. This version has local checks, not an independent security audit. A hosted copy also exposes ordinary connection data to its web host; the hosting privacy facts must be verified before publication.
