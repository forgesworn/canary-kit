Privacy & what this protects

Pairing works in memory, without a vault. Unsaved pairs are cleared when you leave the tab or after five minutes without interaction. Keep on this phone saves nicknames and pair secrets encrypted in this browser. No account, analytics, message service, relay, location access or microphone is used.

On an HTTPS site, the QR is a private setup link that a normal phone camera can open. Its secret is after #, outside the request sent to the host. Canary removes it from the current address and consumes it in memory before showing your words. Camera software, browser history sync or extensions could still keep a copy; no one-time use or revocation is promised. Keep it private. In-app QR scanning uses your camera only when you ask. Frames are decoded on this phone, are not recorded or uploaded, and capture stops when you finish, cancel or leave. The QR contains a shared secret: keep it private.

Device unlock requires supported encryption-key functionality from your browser and passkey provider; otherwise use a passphrase to save. Your provider may sync its credentials under your platform settings. We do not receive the passkey or unlock key. Device-only vaults have no passphrase recovery until you add it in Recovery & device unlock. Encrypted backups require that recovery passphrase. Losing device unlock or browser data without a backup can lose your pairs.

Encryption protects a locked vault or a stolen backup. It cannot protect an unlocked compromised phone, malicious browser extensions, screen capture, a tampered website or a live relay. Only use a trusted address or copy. Both holders can derive either side’s words. If a secret is exposed, pair again privately; removing a local entry does not revoke the other person’s copy.

This does not certify identity, safety or a past meeting. Duress needs an agreed human response; no emergency service is provided. This version has local checks, not an independent security audit. A hosted copy exposes ordinary connection data to its web host; see the hosting notice below.
