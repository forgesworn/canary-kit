Privacy & what this protects

Connect protects and saves progress in an encrypted local vault. Each person needs their own saved copy. No publisher account, app analytics, message service, relay, location access or microphone is used. The HTTPS app caches only the public page, manifest and icons for offline use. Both devices must already have the app available before setting up without internet. Browser storage can still be cleared; keep an encrypted recovery backup. Close all Signet Vouch windows and reopen to apply a downloaded app update.

For offline Connect, both devices display and read public codes with their front cameras. Each code contains a temporary public key, session identifier and random-number commitment. After saving the other device’s code, a device releases its committed random number. These public codes contain no private key, name or shared pair secret. Each device derives that secret locally. Both people compare four fixed words together and save separately; one phone cannot prove the other person confirmed or saved. Setup has no countdown. Scanning pauses after a minute and can resume the same saved connection.

For moving QRs, both devices need this app version. Public invitations/receiving requests and recipient-encrypted replies/copies can travel in both directions over a trusted video call when a second screen is available. A single phone can use the chosen call/chat app’s public-link or encrypted-file handoff. No publisher relay or message endpoint is introduced. The complete SHA-256 frame digest rejects incomplete/mixed data; it does not authenticate the other person. Compare the fixed pair/import code together. Call, screen-sharing and recording providers and participants may see or retain the public/ciphertext frames and timing. Share only the transfer QR, never your vault, rotating call words or recovery screen. Your own duress words are visible in Speak; concealment is not guaranteed. Dedicated transfer scanning and partial assembly are bounded to three minutes and stop on leaving or lock; ordinary offline Connect still pauses after a minute. Selected text/JSON transfer files are processed locally without upload; review decrypted people before importing.

Unfinished connection keys, random numbers, pinned codes and unconfirmed pairs are saved inside the encrypted vault and recovery backup. Completing or removing a connection removes that session from the active vault, not old backups or copies elsewhere. A device without a camera can show its QR and open the other device’s public code file, then show its updated QR. File sharing is your deliberate OS/app action; choose an offline transfer if you want the whole exchange offline. Those apps may use the internet or retain files. No public code file contains the shared secret.

Public codes can link observations of one exchange. Normal cameras can open their HTTPS links. Links use fragments after #, outside server requests, and the app removes them from the address. Cameras, screenshots, history sync, extensions and chosen apps may retain links; removing a fragment does not erase their copies. In-app camera frames are decoded on this device with no recording or upload. Capture stops when you finish reading, lock or leave the view; a late permission grant is stopped too. The older invitation/encrypted-reply QR exchange remains under Other ways to connect. The advanced recovery QR/manual pair contains its shared secret, must stay private and stays in memory until kept; it clears on backgrounding or after five minutes without interaction.

Remote call setup sends only a public invitation and a recipient-encrypted reply through a chat app you choose. Compare the fixed pair code on an already trusted call; the invitation key alone does not identify its owner. Pending invitations and replies are saved inside your encrypted vault. Invitations have no automatic expiry; remove ones you abandon. Shared links and clipboard contents may be retained by the apps you choose. Device unlock requires supported encryption-key functionality from your browser and passkey provider; otherwise use a passphrase to save. Your provider may sync its credentials under your platform settings. We do not receive the passkey or unlock key. Device-only vaults have no passphrase recovery until you add it in Recovery & device unlock. Encrypted backups require that recovery passphrase. Losing device unlock or browser data without a backup can lose your pairs.

Encryption protects a locked vault or a stolen backup. It cannot protect an unlocked compromised phone, malicious browser extensions, screen capture, a tampered website or a live relay. Only use a trusted address or copy. Both holders can derive either side’s words. If a secret is exposed, pair again privately; removing a local entry does not revoke the other person’s copy.

Optional contact notes record how you checked someone, accounts you observed and matching spoken checks. These are your private notes, not independent platform verification. Vouches use a separate signing key per group. For a new named vouch, the holder approves the agreed group nickname in the signed request before replying; the voucher signs that same name with the meeting method and dates. This shared name is disclosed to the holder and, when the holder chooses to present that vouch, to the keeper. Recipients can copy and retain it. Your private address-book aliases are separate and are not automatically put in the signed statement. Older vouches without an agreed name remain meeting records. These signed vouches, private names, chosen vouchers, dated checks and independent pending exchanges stay inside the encrypted vault. The roster combines this device’s saved keys, labels and past evidence; it is not a live membership list. Vouch status uses the signed dates and withdrawals received on this device, with no automatic spoken-pair or messaging-account binding. These records are included in recovery backups. Creating or importing a group makes no Nostr account and contacts no relay. Signet Vouch does not sign into or connect to a Signet service; the product name does not create an account integration.

Group cards and verification cards reveal their public information to whoever receives them. Vouches and check handoffs are encrypted for their intended group key; sharing is a separate action through your camera, clipboard or chosen app. Their software may retain links. Recipients can copy proofs. Offline checks know only the withdrawals this phone has received, and the keeper reviews the signed statement before keeping the key-check evidence. It does not automatically admit or verify an account elsewhere. A lost group key without recovery needs new trust decisions.

Add another device copies selected completed pairs and their contact notes into a separately protected vault. A 15-minute public QR request identifies the receiving encryption key; the copy is encrypted for it, and you compare a fixed code before import. Explicit file sharing uses your chosen OS/app, which may retain the encrypted file. Pending device keys and outgoing encrypted copies are saved inside this vault and its recovery backup. Imports keep existing contacts and unlock protection; group keys and unfinished exchanges are excluded. The original device is kept, with no automatic sync, deletion or revocation. Expiry is an app acceptance limit, not deletion of an exported file or an old backup. This does not certify identity, safety or a past meeting. Duress needs an agreed human response; no emergency service is provided. This version has local checks, not an independent security audit. A hosted copy exposes ordinary connection data to its web host; see the hosting notice below.

Website hosting & privacy contact

Signet Vouch is published by the Canary Kit project (ForgeSworn). Privacy questions, complaints and rights requests can be sent privately to the project maintainers through GitHub’s private report route. That separate service requires a GitHub account. Never include setup links, live words or passphrases in a report.

The HTTPS copy and its public app files are hosted by OpenAI Sites. The host processes connection/request metadata, such as IP and device information, and automatically counts visits and page views. Its Cloudflare security layer may set the __cf_bm bot-protection cookie, which Cloudflare documents as expiring after 30 minutes of inactivity. The purpose is to deliver, secure and operate this static page, including aggregate traffic statistics, on the basis of legitimate interests. Signet Vouch adds no advertising, tracking SDK or message backend. Pair secrets, names and vault passphrases are processed locally by the app; they are not included in its server requests.

Hosting data is processed by OpenAI and its service providers under the applicable hosting data terms. Retention follows the service term and time needed to complete applicable obligations, including deletion; we do not promise a fixed log lifetime or UK-only storage. The applicable terms describe transfer safeguards, including UK provisions.
